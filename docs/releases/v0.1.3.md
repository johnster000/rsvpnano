# v0.1.3

## Reader controls

- Pausing on the LCD 3.49, AMOLED 2.16 and AMOLED 2.41 shows a control bar
  with Menu, Rewind (sentence), speed down/up with the current WPM, and Resume.
  It hides as soon as reading starts.
- Press and hold the word to read: reading starts about 0.14 s into a still
  press and stops on the current word the moment you let go, whatever the
  pause setting. Resume reads hands-free; a tap anywhere stops it, following
  the pause setting. Swipes still scrub and change speed.
- Swiping sideways while paused scrubs by sentence instead of by word. The
  screen shows the end of the previous sentence, the sentence you would resume
  from (marked in the accent color) and the start of the next one, in the 33 px
  strike with a book progress line. Each 60 px of travel moves one sentence;
  letting go starts reading at the beginning of the marked sentence.
  Right-to-left and vertical books, and page mode, keep the page preview.
- While paused, a strip above the word shows the chapter, progress, battery and
  a book progress bar, following the paused visibility settings.
- Layouts with the control bar no longer draw the `<<` rewind hint or keep its
  hidden tap zone while reading; Rewind lives on the bar. A tap anywhere stops
  hands-free reading.
- Back buttons show an arrow, and the resume card shows a play icon.
- Watch layouts and vertical CJK books keep their gesture-only reader.

## Buttons

- On the LCD 3.49, a short press of BOOT switches to the next installed theme
  on any screen and saves it; holding BOOT still enters standby. BOOT no
  longer plays or pauses. Themes come from `/themes` on the SD card; copy the
  repository's `themes` folder there to get light, dark and accent variants.

## Menus

- The library is a list: title, author, progress and a progress line per book,
  with the open book marked. It opens scrolled to that book; drag it or use the
  up and down buttons to page.
- The Read screen labels all of its actions: Chapters, Library and Typeface.
  Typeface replaces the bare language icon that opened per-book fonts.
- The LCD 3.49 menus drop the on-screen power button; a long PWR press powers
  off, and the menu content uses the freed space on the right.
- Buttons, tabs, settings and toggles highlight while pressed, and a slow press
  still counts as long as the finger stays put.

## Fixes

- AMOLED 2.16 and 2.41 can reach Settings, Device, Focus and power-off again
  through tabs across the top.
- The reading position is written to the card on pause, after seeks, when
  opening the menu and every two minutes of playback, not only at chapter
  boundaries.
- Storage checks no longer leave the UI drawing with a freed theme or font.
- Sentence-end detection no longer reads a freed word buffer at cache-window
  boundaries.
- Long `.txt` lines split between words, and chapter titles are cut at a
  character boundary.
- Failed settings writes back off instead of rewriting NVS on every loop.
- The LCD 3.49 reports a lift only after 100 ms without contact, so a resting
  finger no longer flickers out when the touch controller returns empty frames.
- Page mode lays out again after Reader layout changes.
- Page layouts keep at least 0.28 em between words; the compact strike's own
  space is about 0.2 em, which ran words together on the LCD.

## Forks

- Firmware, OTA, catalogs and the web companion follow the building fork's
  owner. See [Running your own fork](../forking.md).

Host checks cover the control layout, menu navigation at 480x480 and
600x450, and the fixed reading-loop and indexer cases. The control bar's
touch feel and the 2.16/2.41 menus still need confirmation on hardware.
